How to measure

  1. Bust. Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping it level across your back.
  2. Waist. Measure around the narrowest part of your torso, usually just above the belly button.
  3. Hips. Measure around the fullest part of your hips and bottom, with your feet together.
  4. Keep the tape snug but not tight, and stand relaxed. Breathe out before you read the number.
